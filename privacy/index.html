Privacy

Your files never leave your browser

This is not a policy promise about servers we control carefully. There is no analysis backend: the audit runs as JavaScript on your machine.

Architecture

No backend

Analysis runs as JavaScript in your browser. There is no server that receives your optimization tables.

Files

No uploads

Reports are read in memory and discarded when you close the tab. Nothing is stored on our side.

Product

Privacy-friendly analytics

No accounts, no cookies, nothing tied to your EA, parameters or results. We only count visits and how often each feature is used, and the statistics we see do not identify anyone.

What we do not collect

  • No file uploads. Your MT5 XML/CSV/HTML reports are read in memory and discarded when you close the tab.
  • No accounts, no registration and no tracking cookies. Nothing we measure can be tied back to your files.
  • We do not see your EA parameters, optimization results or strategy logic.

What stays only on your device

The browser may store locally (never sent to us): your color theme, language preference, and the minimum gates you configure — so you do not have to re-enter them. You can clear them by wiping site data for this domain.

What we measure: anonymous visits and feature use

We use GoatCounter on every page, and Cloudflare Web Analytics on every page except the app, to count how many people visit the site and how often each feature is used: for example, “an analysis was run”, “it failed” or “a .set was downloaded”. What is sent is the page visited, the page you came from, the screen width and, in the app, the name of that event. Neither uses cookies or follows you across websites, and neither can see your files, EA parameters or results. As with any web request, these services receive your IP address; according to their policies, they do not use it to identify you. The GoatCounter script is served from orometra.com itself, so no page runs code loaded from its servers.

What we cannot avoid

The host that serves the website (GitHub Pages) logs, like any web server, basic data about each request: IP address, time and browser. It is unavoidable when serving a page and unrelated to the content of your optimization files.

If you write to us

You can reach us at hello@orometra.com. If you write, we only use your address to reply. Please do not attach your optimization files unless you want us to look at a specific problem.

Who is responsible and your rights

  • Controller: Pol Lázaro. Contact: hello@orometra.com.
  • Personal data processed: the IP address and basic browser data received by the host and the analytics services when you visit the site, and your email address if you write to us. Your MT5 files are never received.
  • Purpose and legal basis: measuring how the site is used in order to improve it, and replying to your messages. The basis is our legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR); in the case of emails, also the fact that you chose to write.
  • Recipients: GitHub (hosting), Cloudflare and GoatCounter (analytics). GitHub and Cloudflare are US companies and may process data outside the EU under the safeguards they offer.
  • Retention: emails are kept for as long as needed to answer them and follow up; analytics are kept as aggregate statistics.
  • Your rights: you can request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction or portability by writing to hello@orometra.com, and lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).
