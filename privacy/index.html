Privacy

Your files never leave your browser

This is not a policy promise about servers we control carefully. There is no analysis backend: the audit runs as JavaScript on your machine.

Architecture

No backend

Analysis runs as JavaScript in your browser. There is no server that receives your optimization tables.

Files

No uploads

Reports are read in memory and discarded when you close the tab. Nothing is stored on our side.

Product

Privacy-friendly analytics

No accounts, no cookies, nothing tied to your EA, parameters or results. We only see anonymous visits and how often each feature is used.

What we do not collect

  • No file uploads. Your MT5 XML/CSV/HTML reports are read in memory and discarded when you close the tab.
  • No accounts, no registration, no tracking cookies, and nothing that identifies you or ties back to your files.
  • We do not see your EA parameters, optimization results or strategy logic.

What stays only on your device

The browser may store locally (never sent to us): your color theme, language preference, and the minimum gates you configure — so you do not have to re-enter them. You can clear them by wiping site data for this domain.

What we measure: anonymous visits and feature use

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics and GoatCounter to count how many people visit the site and how often each feature is used: for example, “an analysis was run”, “it failed” or “a .set was downloaded”. Only the name of that event is sent. Neither uses cookies or follows you across sites, and neither can see your files, EA parameters or results — those never leave your browser.

What we cannot avoid

Any host that serves the website (for example GitHub Pages) logs basic HTTP requests: IP address, time, browser. That is normal for every website and is unrelated to the contents of your optimization files.

If you write to us

You can reach us at hello@orometra.com. If you write, we only use your address to reply. Please do not attach your optimization files unless you want us to look at a specific problem.
