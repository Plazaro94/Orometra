Methodology

How the engine decides

Orometra does not re-rank your MT5 table. It measures whether strong results sit inside stable parameter regions — and how much evidence supports that claim.

  1. 01 Gates Absolute minima in both periods
  2. 02 Quality Rebuilt without Result
  3. 03 Neighborhood Local stability measured
  4. 04 Plateau Connected good regions
  5. 05 Representative Maximin, not the peak
  6. 06 Unseen Optional clean check

1. Absolute gates before rankings

A configuration enters the analysis only if it clears minimums you set (profit factor, drawdown, trades, positive profit) in both periods. Percentiles always invent a “top 5%”, even when everything loses money. Absolute floors can say: there is nothing here.

2. Quality without the Result column

MT5’s Result column is whatever criterion you optimized for. It changes meaning between runs and is contaminated by selection. Orometra rebuilds quality from objective columns MT5 always exports: profit factor, recovery, Sharpe, drawdown and trade count. Combined quality is the minimum of in-sample and forward — not the average.

3. Plateaus, not peaks

Neighboring configurations are compared in parameter steps. A plateau is a connected region where the lower quartile of the neighborhood stays good and almost all neighbors pass your gates. The representative is chosen by maximin: the point whose worst neighbor is as good as possible — the center of the ridge, not the tip.

4. Evidence strength, not GO / NO-GO

The verdict grades how strong the evidence is (strong / moderate / weak / insufficient). It does not tell you to trade. It can also say the dataset is too small to conclude anything — which is different from “no plateau found”.

5. What we deliberately do not claim

  • We do not run classic CSCV / published PBO: MT5 optimization exports lack per-configuration equity curves.
  • We do not run White’s Reality Check or Hansen’s SPA for the same reason.
  • Forward figures already participate in selection, so they are slightly contaminated; the cleanest check is an unseen period you fix criteria for in advance.
  • A plateau is evidence of parameter stability in your sample — not proof of future edge.
  • Coverage on “seen levels” is not coverage of the .set search range; drop the optimization .set to contrast them.
  • “Trades per fitted parameter” is an evidence-density check, not formal degrees of freedom. Sharpe uses Lo (2002) chance thresholds — not classic Bailey deflated Sharpe as approval.
