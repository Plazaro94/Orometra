Methodology

How the engine decides

We do not re-rank your MT5 table. We look for stable zones — and tell you how strong the evidence is.

  1. 01 Gates Your minima in both periods
  2. 02 Quality Rebuilt without Result
  3. 03 Neighborhood Do neighbors also hold?
  4. 04 Plateau Connected good zones
  5. 05 Pick Center of the plateau, not the peak
  6. 06 Unseen Optional clean check
01

Minima before rankings

A setup only enters if it clears your floors (PF, drawdown, trades, profit) in both periods. Rankings always invent a “top 5%” — even when everything loses.

02

Quality without Result

MT5’s Result column is whatever you optimized for. We rebuild quality from objective exports (PF, recovery, Sharpe, drawdown, trades). Score = worse of in-sample and forward — not the average.

03

Plateaus, not peaks

Isolated spikes are rejected. A plateau is a zone where neighbors also clear your minima — we pick its center, the point whose worst neighbor is still solid, not the single tallest spike.

04

Evidence strength — not a trade signal

The verdict is strong / moderate / weak / insufficient. It never means “go trade”. Too little data is not the same as “no plateau”.

05

Stress-tested on your actual trades

The grid only has aggregated metrics — not enough for a real Monte Carlo. Drop your chosen configuration's backtest report and unlock three checks the grid alone can't give you: a Monte Carlo over your real trades, a sample-size check, and a cost-stress test for worse-than-optimized spread or slippage.

06

What we do not claim

  • A plateau is stability in your sample — not proof of future edge.
  • We do not invent classic PBO / Reality Check / SPA: MT5 optimization files lack the equity curves those tests need.
  • Forward already helps select, so it is slightly “contaminated”; the cleanest check is a true unseen period.
  • We do not tell you to buy, sell, or fund an EA. Evidence ≠ permission to trade.
  • With many parameters or a genetic (non-grid) search, neighborhood and plateau size are capped conservatively — it may understate support, never invent it.
