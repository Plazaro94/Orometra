No starling flies alone

Each one steers by its nearest neighbors, and that is how the whole flock holds together. Orometra judges your configurations the same way: it only trusts one that works alongside those around it.

    Doesn't trust the Result column

    It measures quality with objective metrics, not with what you optimized for.

  3. 03

    Checks the neighbors

    Inside the plateau it picks what holds in both periods alongside its neighbors, not the peak.

  4. 04

    Checks it against the forward

    What holds up in the forward moves up; what falls apart moves down.

  5. 05

    Estimates how much could be chance

    It warns you if your result doesn't beat what pure chance would produce.

  6. 06

    Tests itself

    It checks that the recommendation doesn't depend on its own settings.

Frequently asked questions

Something else? Write to us at hello@orometra.com.

Which files do I need?

The XML exports of your MT5 optimization: the results tab (in-sample) and, if you enabled forward, the forward tab too. The .opt file does not work. Step-by-step guide.

How much does it cost?

Right now, nothing: in this phase Orometra is free, with no sign-up and no card. If paid features come later, they will be announced in advance.

What happens to my data?

Nothing leaves your computer: the analysis runs in your browser. We only count, anonymously, visits and how often each feature is used. Details in the privacy page.

What does it not do?

It does not predict the market, guarantee returns or turn a weak EA into a good one. With genetic optimization it is more cautious, and tells you so.
