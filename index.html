What you get

Stable regions, not luck

Finds connected neighborhoods where neighboring parameters also work. An isolated spike is rejected, even if it topped your MT5 table.

Top 3 with reasons

Three concrete configurations, neighbor support, out-of-sample behaviour, and the risks that still apply.

Evidence strength

Strong, moderate, weak, or insufficient. Orometra measures what your data can support — it does not promise future profits.

How it works

  1. Export your MT5 Optimization report (XML). Add Forward if you used it.
  2. Drop the files in the app. In-sample and forward are detected by content.
  3. Read the verdict, plateaus, and rejected peaks — then export a .set to refine in MT5.

What Orometra does not do

  • It does not predict the market or guarantee returns.
  • It does not pick the highest-profit pass by default.
  • It does not turn a weak EA into a strong one.
