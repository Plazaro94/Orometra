Product

What you get

  1. 01

    Stable regions, not luck

    Finds connected neighborhoods where neighboring parameters also work, shown as a real 3D surface — not this page's illustration. An isolated spike is rejected, even if it topped your MT5 table.

  2. 02

    Top 3 with reasons

    Three concrete configurations, neighbor support, out-of-sample behaviour, and the risks that still apply.

  3. 03

    Evidence strength

    Strong, moderate, weak, or insufficient. Orometra measures what your data can support — it does not promise future profits.

  4. 04

    Robustness under real costs

    Drop your backtest report on the unseen period and get a Monte Carlo over your actual trades, a sample-size check, and a cost-stress table — no install, straight from the trade list MT5 already gives you.

Risk

Three mistakes it prevents

  • Trusting the #1 row

    The top of an MT5 table is often an isolated peak. Neighbors and out-of-sample behaviour stay invisible until you audit the surface.

  • Averaging away risk

    Orometra scores by the worse period and by maximin neighborhoods — not by an average that hides a fragile edge.

  • Confusing evidence with a GO

    A strong grade means the data can support a stable region. It is not permission to trade or a forecast of future profit.

Workflow

How it works

Optimize. Export. Audit.

  1. 1 Optimize with Forward on. Split in-sample and out-of-sample in the Strategy Tester before you run the optimization, not after — it's the only way the forward period is genuinely one you never used to choose. Export both reports (XML).
  2. 2 Drop the files in the app. In-sample and forward are detected by content.
  3. 3 Read the verdict, plateaus, and rejected peaks. Export a .set to refine in MT5, or drop a single backtest report to see Monte Carlo and cost stress on your actual trades.

How the engine decides — gates, plateaus, maximin — is documented in the methodology.

Limits

What Orometra does not do

  • It does not predict the market or guarantee returns.
  • It does not pick the highest-profit pass by default.
  • It does not turn a weak EA into a strong one.
