Frequently asked questions

Which files do I need?

The XML exports of your MT5 optimization: the results tab (in-sample) and, if you enabled forward, the forward tab too. The .opt file does not work.

Do I need the forward period?

No, but it is highly recommended. Without it, Orometra analyzes the in-sample only and the evidence cannot go beyond weak.

Is it free?

Yes. No sign-up and no limits.

What happens to my data?

Nothing leaves your computer: the analysis runs in your browser. We only count visits, anonymously. Details in the privacy page.

Why not just ask an AI?

Testing thousands of combinations always produces some good-looking result by pure chance. Orometra applies a fixed, tested and reproducible criterion: same data, same answer.

What does it not do?

It does not predict the market, guarantee returns or turn a weak EA into a good one. With genetic optimization it is more cautious, and tells you so.

And after that?

Test the configuration on a period you have not used. Orometra reads that backtest report and tells you whether the result is normal for your EA, with a Monte Carlo on your own trades.
