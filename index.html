Frequently asked questions

Something else? Write to us at hello@orometra.com.

Which files do I need?

The XML that MT5 exports with your optimization results: right-click the table and export to XML. If you enabled forward, export that table too. The .opt file does not work. Step-by-step guide.

What if I did not enable forward?

You can still analyze it, but the evidence will not go beyond “weak”: without data the optimization never saw, a robust plateau cannot be told apart from a lucky one. Next time, enable it in the tester settings (a quarter or a third of the period).

Does it work with genetic optimization?

Yes. Since the genetic algorithm does not test every combination, plateaus are measured conservatively: the result may fall short, but it never invents support. The report tells you.

Does it work with my EA?

With any MetaTrader 5 EA, on any symbol and timeframe: Orometra does not read your code, only the results table. MetaTrader 4 is not supported for now.

How much does it cost?

Nothing: in this phase Orometra is free, with no sign-up and no card. If paid features ever come, they will be announced in advance.

What happens to my data?

Nothing leaves your computer: the analysis runs in your browser. We only count, anonymously, visits and how often each feature is used. Details in the privacy page.

Will it tell me whether my EA will make money?

No. It tells you how much your data supports the chosen configuration; no level means “trade it”. It does not predict the market or turn a weak EA into a good one, and if your backtest is optimistic (spread, history quality), so is the analysis.

Why not just ask an AI?

It will give you a convincing explanation, different every time. Orometra is a fixed calculation: same files, same result; it knows how to say “insufficient”, and your parameters never leave your computer. Full comparison.
